Periodic Matrix of Online Threats & Safety Actions

Just as elements are organized by properties, digital risks and safety habits follow clear patterns. Use the color-coded matrix below to identify common forms of online harm and the exact steps needed to stop them.

Category Common Online Threat Warning Signs Recommended Safety Action
Risk 01 Cyber-Harassment Direct Threat Repeated Hostile Messages Sending insults, slurs, or threats via group chats, comments, or direct messages. Escalating Frequency Targeted replies across multiple platforms or anonymous accounts. Mute, Screenshot, Block Do not reply. Capture full-screen evidence with timestamps, then block and report the sender.
Risk 02 Social Exclusion Group Harm Ostracism & Rumor Spreading Intentionally kicking someone out of class chats or posting edited memes to humiliate them. Coordinated Tagging Peers sharing altered photos or private screenshots without consent. Bystander Intervention Refuse to forward hurtful posts, report the content, and reach out privately to support the target.
Risk 03 Doxxing & Outing Privacy Breach Exposing Private Info Publishing someone's home address, phone number, school schedule, or private secrets online. Oversharing & Public Profiles Visible geotags, public friend lists, or leaked personal photos. Lock Down Privacy Settings Set accounts to private, disable location tags on posts, and request immediate takedowns.
Risk 04 Impersonation Identity Theft Fake Profiles & Account Hijacking Creating fake accounts using a student's name and photo to post damaging content or phish friends. Suspicious Login Alerts Unexpected password reset emails or duplicate profiles messaging classmates. Enable 2FA & Report Impostor Turn on Two-Factor Authentication (2FA), update passwords, and submit an impersonation report.

Step-by-Step Response Checklist

  1. Stay Calm and Step Away: Log out or silence notifications to avoid emotional retaliation.
  2. Preserve Digital Evidence: Save uncropped screenshots showing usernames, dates, and URLs.
  3. Use Platform Safety Tools: Restrict comments, filter offensive keywords, and block abusive accounts.
  4. Notify Trusted Authorities: Share evidence with a parent, school guidance counselor, or platform moderator.
