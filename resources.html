Where to Get Help

If you or someone you know is experiencing cyberbullying, online harassment, or account extortion, contact these official support channels immediately:

Organization / Agency Support Provided Official Website / Link
PNP Anti-Cybercrime Group (Philippines) Reporting cyberbullying, online identity theft, and cybercrime incidents. Visit PNP Anti-Cybercrime Group Portal
Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) Cybersecurity awareness advisories and digital safety programs in the Philippines. Visit DICT Official Website
StopBullying.gov Research-backed guides on preventing cyberbullying and supporting youth. Read StopBullying.gov Cyberbullying Guide
UNICEF Cyberbullying Resource Hub Global youth guide on how to stop cyberbullying and protect mental well-being. Read UNICEF Cyberbullying Handbook
