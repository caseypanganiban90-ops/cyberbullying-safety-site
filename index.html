Why Online Safety Matters Today

Social media, messaging apps, and online gaming connect students every day, but they also expose users to cyberbullying, impersonation, and privacy leaks. Understanding how digital harm happens—and how to respond safely—empowers students to protect themselves and support their peers.

  • Digital Awareness: Recognizing harmful online behavior before it escalates.
  • Account Security: Locking down personal profiles, passwords, and privacy settings.
  • Positive Bystander Action: Reporting abuse and supporting targeted classmates without spreading harm.
